  \liturgyhead{Opening Hymn}

  \hymntitle{Yes, Heaven Is the Prize}

  \hymnverse{1.}{Yes, heaven is the prize\\
    My soul shall strive to gain;\\
    One glimpse of paradise\\
    Repays a life of pain.}

  \hymnchorus{'Tis heaven, 'tis heaven,\\
    Yes, heaven is the prize. \emph{(twice)}}

  \hymnverse{2.}{Yes, heaven is the prize\\
    When sorrows press around;\\
    Look up beyond the skies,\\
    Where hope and strength are found.}

  \hymnverse{3.}{Yes, heaven is the prize,\\
    My soul, O think of this;\\
    All earthly goods despise\\
    For such a crown of pain.}

  \hymnverse{4.}{Yes, heaven is the prize,\\
    O! it is not hard to gain;\\

  \hymnverse{5.}{Yes, heaven is the prize,\\

  \liturgyhead{Introduction}

  \priest{In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \liturgyhead{Penitential Rite}

  \priest{Brethren, let us acknowledge our sins, and so prepare ourselves to
    celebrate the sacred mysteries.}

  \rubric{A brief pause for silence follows, then all recite the formula of
    general confession.}

  \allsay{I confess to Almighty God and to you, my brothers and sisters, that
    I have greatly sinned, in my thoughts and in my words, in what I have done
    and in what I have failed to do, through my fault, through my fault,
    through my most grievous fault. Therefore, I ask blessed Mary ever Virgin,
    all the Angels and Saints, and you, my brothers and sisters, to pray for me
    to the Lord our God.}

  \priest{May Almighty God have mercy on us, forgive us our sins, and bring us
    to life everlasting.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \priest{Lord, have mercy.}
  \allsay{Lord, have mercy.}

  \priest{Christ, have mercy.}
  \allsay{Christ, have mercy.}

  \priest{Lord, have mercy.}
  \allsay{Lord, have mercy.}

  \liturgyhead{Opening Prayer}

  \priest{Let us pray. O God, whose nature is always to forgive and show mercy,
    we humbly implore you for your servant, Francis Atsekhameh, whom you have
    called to journey to you; and since he hoped and believed in you, grant
    that he may be led to our true homeland to delight in its everlasting joys.
    Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who lives and reigns with you, in
    the unity of the Holy Spirit, one God for ever and ever.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \liturgypart{Liturgy of the Word}

  \liturgyhead{First Reading}

  \rubric{A reading from the Book of the Prophet Isaiah \quad 25:6--9}

  On this mountain, the Lord of hosts will prepare for all peoples a banquet of
  rich food. On this mountain He will remove the mourning veil covering all
  peoples, and the shroud enwrapping all nations. He will destroy death for
  ever. The Lord will wipe away tears from every cheek; He will take away his
  people's shame everywhere on earth, for the Lord has said so. That day, it
  will be said: See, this is our God in whom we hoped for salvation; the Lord is
  the one in whom we hoped. We exult and we rejoice that He has saved us.

  \vspace{2mm}
  \versicle{The word of the Lord.}
  \allsay{Thanks be to God.}

  \liturgyhead{Responsorial Psalm}

  \rubric{Psalm 23:1--3, 4--5, 6 \quad (R.\ v.\,1)}

  \respond{The Lord is my shepherd; there is nothing I shall want.}

  \hymnverse{1.}{The Lord is my shepherd;\\
    there is nothing I shall want.\\
    Fresh and green are the pastures\\
    where he gives me repose.\\
    Near restful waters he leads me;\\
    he revives my soul. \psalmcue}

  \hymnverse{2.}{He guides me along the right path,\\
    for the sake of his name.\\
    Though I should walk in the valley\\
    of the shadow of death,\\
    no evil would I fear, for you are with me.\\
    Your crook and your staff will give me comfort. \psalmcue}

  \hymnverse{3.}{You have prepared a table before me\\
    in the sight of my foes.\\
    My head you have anointed with oil;\\
    my cup is overflowing. \psalmcue}

  \hymnverse{4.}{Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me\\
    all the days of my life.\\
    In the Lord's own house shall I dwell\\
    for length of days unending. \psalmcue}

  \liturgyhead{Second Reading}

  \rubric{A reading from the Letter of St.\ Paul to the Romans \quad 6:3--9}

  \hymntitle{Alive or dead, we belong to the Lord}

  Brethren: All of us who have been baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized
  into his death. We were buried therefore with him by baptism into death, so
  that as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, we too
  might walk in newness of life. For if we have been united with him in a death
  like his, we shall certainly be united with him in a resurrection like his.
  We know that our former man was crucified with him so that the sinful body
  might be destroyed, and we might no longer be enslaved to sin. For he who has
  died is freed from sin. But if we have died with Christ, we believe that we
  shall also live with him. For we know that Christ being raised from the dead
  will never die again; death no longer has dominion over him.

  \vspace{2mm}
  \versicle{The word of the Lord.}
  \allsay{Thanks be to God.}

  \liturgyhead{Gospel Acclamation}

  \allverse{Alleluia, alleluia!\\
    Our homeland is in heaven, and from heaven comes the Saviour we are
    waiting for, the Lord Jesus Christ. Alleluia!}

  \liturgyhead{Gospel}

  \rubric{All stand for the Gospel.}

  \priest{The Lord be with you.}
  \allsay{And with your spirit.}

  \priest{A reading from the holy Gospel according to John \quad 11:21--27}
  \allsay{Glory be to you, Lord.}

  \vspace{1mm}
  At that time, Martha said to Jesus, ``If you had been here, my brother would
  not have died, but I know that, even now, whatever you ask of God, He will
  grant you.'' ``Your brother,'' said Jesus to her, ``will rise again.'' Martha
  said, ``I know that he will rise again at the resurrection on the last day.''
  Jesus said, ``I am the resurrection and the life. If anyone believes in me,
  even though he dies, he will live; and whoever lives and believes in me will
  never die. Do you believe this?'' ``Yes, Lord,'' she said, ``I believe that
  you are the Christ, the Son of God, the one who was to come into this world.''

  \vspace{2mm}
  \versicle{The Gospel of the Lord.}
  \allsay{Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.}

  \liturgyhead{Homily}

  \rubric{All sit.}

  \liturgyhead{Prayer of the Faithful}

  \rubric{All stand.}

  \priest{Christ our Lord, by His death and victory over sin, has opened the
    gates of heaven for his people. Let us pray that the soul of our brother,
    father, father-in-law and uncle, Francis Atsekhameh, may come to his true
    home in heaven, and that God may in time reunite us all there.}

  \respond{Hear our humble prayer; mercy on your people, Lord.}

  \intercession{1.}{For the Church:}{Let us pray for our Holy Father, Pope
    Francis, our Archbishop, Augustine Akubeze, the Order of Bishops, the
    Clergy and Religious, that they may be strengthened by the power of the
    Holy Spirit in the spread of the Gospel. We pray, O Lord.}

  \intercession{2.}{For the repose of his soul:}{Let us pray for the repose of
    the soul of Francis Atsekhameh, that Almighty God, who called him to His
    embrace, may forgive his sins and welcome him into the company of the
    Angels and Saints. We pray, O Lord.}

  \intercession{3.}{For all the mourners:}{Let us pray for all those who are
    left to bear the loss of Francis Atsekhameh, particularly his children and
    relatives. May they be comforted and strengthened by our friendship and
    prayers as they face the future without the physical presence of their dear
    one. We pray, O Lord.}

  \intercession{4.}{For the sick and suffering:}{Let us pray for those who are
    sick at home and in hospitals, for those who suffer from recurring
    sickness, those who are under physical and spiritual oppression, and all
    those problems that defy treatment, that Christ, for whom nothing is
    impossible, may grant them total recovery. We pray, O Lord.}

  \intercession{5.}{For all God's people:}{Let us pray that all God's people
    will prepare for the glorious day when Jesus will transform their bodies
    into copies of His own glorious body in heaven. We pray, O Lord.}

  \priest{Let us ask Mary our Mother to intercede for us as we say, Hail
    Mary\ldots}

  \liturgyhead{Concluding Prayer}

  \priest{Lord, in our moment of suffering, comfort us to remember that you are
    always there to help us to victory and a glorious end. We also ask you to
    help comfort, through our prayers, the family of your son, Francis
    Atsekhameh, who suffer this loss. We ask this through Christ our Lord.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \liturgyhead{Offertory Hymn}

  \hymntitle{Trust and Obey}

  \hymnverse{1.}{When we walk with the Lord,\\
    In the light of His Word,\\
    What a glory He sheds on our way.\\
    While we do His goodwill,\\
    He abides with us still,\\
    And with all who will trust and obey.}

  \hymnchorus{Trust and obey, for there's no other way\\
    To be happy in Jesus, but to trust and obey.}

  \hymnverse{2.}{Not a shadow can rise, not a cloud in the skies,\\
    But His smile quickly drives it away;\\
    Not a doubt or a fear, not a sigh or a tear,\\
    Can abide while we trust and obey.}

  \hymnverse{3.}{Not a burden we bear, not a sorrow we share,\\
    But our toil He doth richly repay,\\
    Not a grief or a loss, not a crown or a cross,\\
    But is blessed if we trust and obey.}

  \hymnverse{4.}{\ldots{} the delights of His love,\\
    Until all on the altar we lay,\\
    For the favour He shows,\\
    for the joy He bestows,\\
    Are for them who will trust and obey.}

  \liturgypart{Liturgy of the Holy Eucharist}

  \liturgyhead{Invitation to Prayer}

  \rubric{After the preparation of the gifts, standing at the middle of the
    altar, facing the people, extending and then joining his hands, the Priest
    says:}

  \priest{Pray, brethren, that my sacrifice and yours may be acceptable to God,
    the Almighty Father.}
  \allsay{May the Lord accept the sacrifice at your hands, for the praise and
    glory of His name, for our good and the good of all His holy Church. Amen.}

  \liturgyhead{Prayer over the Gifts}

  \priest{As we humbly present to you these sacrificial offerings, O Lord, for
    the salvation of your servant, Francis Atsekhameh, we beseech your mercy,
    that he, who did not doubt your Son to be his loving Saviour, may find in
    Him a merciful judge; who lives and reigns for ever and ever.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \liturgyhead{The Eucharistic Prayer}

  \priest{The Lord be with you.}
  \allsay{And with your spirit.}

  \priest{Lift up your hearts.}
  \allsay{We lift them up to the Lord.}

  \priest{Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.}
  \allsay{It is right and just.}

  \rubric{The Priest, with hands extended, continues the Preface. At the end he
    joins his hands and concludes the Preface with the people singing or saying
    aloud:}

  \liturgyhead{Sanctus}

  \allverse{Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord God of hosts.\\
    Heaven and earth are full of your glory.\\
    Hosanna in the highest.\\
    Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord.\\
    Hosanna in the highest.}

  \rubric{All kneel while the Priest continues with the Eucharistic Prayer.}

  \liturgyhead{The Doxology and Great Amen}

  \rubric{At the end of the Eucharistic Prayer, the Priest takes the paten with
    the Host and, lifting them up, says or sings:}

  \priest{Through Him, and with Him, and in Him, O God, Almighty Father, in the
    unity of the Holy Spirit, all glory and honour is yours, for ever and ever.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \liturgyhead{The Communion Prayer}

  \priest{At the Saviour's command and formed by divine teaching, we dare to
    say:}

  \rubric{He extends his hands and, together with the people, continues:}

  \allsay{Our Father, who art in heaven\ldots}

  \priest{Deliver us, Lord, we pray\ldots}
  \allsay{For the kingdom, the power and the glory are yours, now and for ever.}

  \liturgyhead{Rite of Peace}

  \priest{Lord Jesus Christ, who said to your Apostles: peace I leave you, my
    peace I give you; look not on our sins, but on the faith of your Church,
    and graciously grant her peace and unity in accordance with your will. Who
    live and reign for ever and ever.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \priest{The peace of the Lord be with you always.}
  \allsay{And with your spirit.}

  \liturgyhead{Breaking of Bread --- Agnus Dei}

  \allverse{Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world,\\
    have mercy on us.\\
    Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world,\\
    have mercy on us.\\
    Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world,\\
    grant us peace.}

  \priest{Behold the Lamb of God, behold Him who takes away the sins of the
    world. Blessed are those who are called to the supper of the Lamb.}
  \allsay{Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my roof, but only
    say the word and my soul shall be healed.}

  \liturgyhead{Communion Hymns}

  \hymntitle{I.\quad It Is Well with My Soul}

  \hymnverse{1.}{When peace like a river attendeth my way,\\
    When sorrow like sea billows roll;\\
    Whatever my lot, thou hast taught me to say,\\
    It is well, it is well with my soul.}

  \hymnchorus{It is well, it is well,\\
    With my soul, with my soul;\\
    It is well, it is well, with my soul.}

  \hymnverse{3.}{Though Satan should buffet,\\
    Though trials should come,\\
    Let this blessed assurance control\ldots}

  \columndivider

  \hymntitle{II.\quad Guide Me, O Thou Great Redeemer}

  \hymnverse{1.}{Guide me, O Thou great Redeemer,\\
    Pilgrim through this barren land;\\
    I am weak but Thou art mighty,\\
    Hold me with Thy powerful hand.}

  \hymnchorus{Bread of heaven, bread of heaven,\\
    Feed me now and evermore. \emph{(twice)}}

  \hymnverse{2.}{We gather, Lord, around your table,\\
    Waiting for the Bread of life.\\
    Make us worthy, make us holy,\\
    Take away all sin and strife.}

  \hymnverse{3.}{Living bread come down from heaven,\\
    He who eats it shall not die.\\
    Come and eat and live for ever,\\
    You shall live with God on high.}

  \liturgyhead{Post Communion Prayer}

  \priest{Grant, we pray, Almighty God, that your servant, Francis Atsekhameh,
    who has journeyed from this world, may by this sacrifice be cleansed and
    freed from sin, and so receive the everlasting joys of the resurrection.
    Through Christ our Lord.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \liturgyhead{Announcements \& Vote of Thanks}

  \liturgyhead{Final Blessing and Dismissal}

  \priest{The Lord be with you.}
  \allsay{And with your spirit.}

  \priest{May Almighty God bless you, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy
    Spirit.}
  \allsay{Amen.}

  \priest{The Mass is ended. Go forth in the peace of Christ.}
  \allsay{Thanks be to God.}

  \liturgyhead{Closing Hymn}

  \hymntitle{Abide with Me}

  \hymnverse{1.}{Abide with me, fast falls the eventide:\\
    The darkness deepens; Lord, with me abide:\\
    When other helpers fail, and comforts flee,\\
    Help of the helpless, O abide with me.}

  \hymnverse{2.}{Swift to its close ebbs out life's little day,\\
    Earth's joys grow dim, its glories pass away,\\
    Change and decay in all around I see:\\
    O Thou who changest not, abide with me.}
