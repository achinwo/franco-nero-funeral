\begin{center}
  \vspace*{2mm}

  % The two faces of the same sheet: this page is set in the cover's faces,
  % not the booklet's, so that the back reads as the front's other side.
  {\coverface\fontsize{11.5}{14}\selectfont\color{primary}
  \coverline[180]{OUR HEARTFELT APPRECIATION}\par}

  \vspace{3mm}
  \diamondrule{16mm}{1.1mm}
  \vspace{4.5mm}

  % Five paragraphs where the page used to carry four lines. Set a size down
  % from the old block and given air between the paragraphs instead of the
  % single unbroken column they would otherwise make: at this measure a
  % centred italic needs the breaks to be seen, or the whole page reads as one
  % grey shape. \parskip rather than blank-line indents -- centred text cannot
  % show an indent.
  \begin{minipage}{0.9\linewidth}
    \centering\coverface\fontsize{9.6}{13}\selectfont\itshape\color{primary}
    \setlength{\parskip}{2.4mm}\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}
    ``As we bid our beloved Husband, Father, Grandfather, Brother, Uncle,
    Cousin and Friend, Sir Francis Onoteagbor Atsekhameh --- our very own
    Franco Nero International --- a final farewell, our hearts are deeply
    filled with gratitude.

    Our profound appreciation goes to everyone who stood by us during this
    difficult time and contributed in one way or another to make the ceremony
    a huge success.

    Your presence, kind gestures, generosity, prayers, words of encouragement
    and all round thoughtfulness has been a great source of strength to us,
    and has reminded us that we are not alone. So we will forever cherish them
    in our hearts.

    We pray that the good Lord will bless and reward you abundantly, grant you
    peace, ease and joy, and repay every kindness shown to our family. May He
    grant you journey mercies back to your various destinations and continue
    to keep you and your families under His loving care.

    Thank you for helping us honour the life and memory of our beloved Father.
    We pray that God will bless you all abundantly, in Jesus' name, Amen.''
  \end{minipage}
